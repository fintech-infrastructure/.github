# Fintech Infrastructure

Open-source payment processing datasets, compatibility-first fee calculators, and switching benchmarks for US businesses.

## Flagship Project

- **[payment-processing-comparison](https://github.com/fintech-infrastructure/payment-processing-comparison)** — Verified pricing and compatibility dataset across 24 payment processors, gateways, ACH platforms, and Merchant of Record services, plus a Python CLI and web calculator.
- **Interactive Calculator & Guides:** [fintech-infrastructure.github.io/payment-processing-comparison](https://fintech-infrastructure.github.io/payment-processing-comparison/)

## Focus Areas

- **Compatibility First:** Merchant country vs. customer country, platform support (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom API), ACH, and tax responsibility.
- **Real Monthly Cost:** Flat-rate vs. Interchange-Plus modeling at your exact monthly volume and average ticket size.
- **Switching Analysis:** Monthly and annual savings when moving off Stripe, Square, or PayPal, with token portability notes.
